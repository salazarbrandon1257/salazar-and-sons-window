Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about window installation, replacement, and repair in Dallas-Fort Worth.

How much does window replacement cost?

Every home is different, so pricing depends on window size, material choice, and the number of windows. We give every homeowner a free, no-obligation estimate with a clear, honest quote and no hidden fees. Call (469) 909-3185 to schedule yours.

What areas do you serve?

We're a family-owned company based in Dallas, TX, and we serve the entire Dallas-Fort Worth area, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Grand Prairie, Irving, and Oak Cliff.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Salazar & Sons Window Co. is licensed and insured, and registered with the City of Dallas Building Inspection Division.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Every installation comes with our lifetime leak warranty. If a window we installed ever leaks because of our installation work, we'll come back and fix it for free, parts and labor included, for as long as you own your home. The window products themselves are also covered by their manufacturer's warranty.

What window brands do you install?

We install windows from trusted manufacturers including Pella, Jeld-Wen, Ply Gem, Westlake, Buena Vista, and Burris, with energy-efficient options built to last.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available through Acorn Finance, with new windows for as low as $99/month for approved customers. Terms depend on approval and eligibility.

Should I repair or replace my windows?

It depends on the damage. If the frame is rotted or the opening is out of standard, we can repair and reframe it so a new window fits clean and seals tight. If your windows are old, drafty, or damaged, replacement with modern energy-efficient windows is usually the better investment. We'll tell you honestly which makes sense during your free estimate.

Do you install windows in new construction?

Yes. We handle new construction and first-time installations from measurement to finishing, ensuring a proper fit and seal every time.

What are your business hours?

We're available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call (469) 909-3185 or request a free estimate online anytime.
