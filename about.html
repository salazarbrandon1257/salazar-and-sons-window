About the Owner

Meet Brandon Salazar

Hi, my name is Brandon Salazar, and I’m the owner of Salazar & Sons Window Co. I’ve lived in Dallas most of my life and spent years working in general construction before transitioning into real estate. Through that experience, I developed a strong knowledge of window installation, repair, and replacement, along with a passion for quality craftsmanship.

At Salazar & Sons Window Co, we take pride in providing honest service, dependable workmanship, and results that homeowners can trust. Customer satisfaction is our top priority, and we’re committed to making sure every project is completed with care and attention to detail.

If you’re looking for reliable window services, please don’t hesitate to call, text, or email us for a free quote. We’d be happy to help bring your vision to life.
